# Approvals

One inbox holds every request that needs a yes from someone else. The person who asks cannot be the person who approves it.

## How a request moves

It starts pending on the first person who should see it. If that step needs everyone listed, it stays open until each of them agrees. One rejection closes it, keeps the comment, and marks the subject as a draft again. The person who asked can cancel. When the deadline passes, the people waiting get one reminder at the halfway point, and the request moves up once. It does not escalate a second time.

A comment is required to reject. A comment is also required to approve a discount above 15 percent. That threshold is on the rule and can be edited.

While a request is pending, asking again for the same thing cancels the old one and starts a new one.

## Rules already on

These are starter rules, marked for Sam to confirm. They can be read on the inbox by management.

| Request | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Quote discount | A sales agent may take up to 5 percent without asking. Above that, the sales manager. Above 15 percent, management as well. Above 30 percent, the super admin as well. Four hours. |
| Sign at install | Management or the super admin. Four hours. |
| Reseller agreement | Management or the super admin. A day. |
| Referral reward | The sales manager or management. A day. |
| Price change | Management, then the super admin. Two days. The person who asked cannot publish it. |
| Move existing services to a new price | The same two steps. Services keep the old price until both yeses are in. |
| Stock count | A difference of 2 units or less is recorded and left. A bigger difference waits for the warehouse, and for finance if the value is over $100. |
| Refund, credit note, write-off, contract change, free installation, retention, a bulk connection change, a purchase, a role change, and deleting a customer | Seeded with the ladder from the plan. They wait until those screens exist. |

A group of requests can be approved together only when every one of them is marked low-risk. None of the money rules are.

Someone can hand their approvals to a colleague for a date range. That hand-off is recorded.

The calendar shows approvals that are due. The bell and the email say when one is requested, decided, or out of time.

# Retention

A termination order opens a retention case before it can be completed. The thirty-day collections step does the same, instead of closing the service immediately. The case stays with the call-center supervisors. Completing the termination needs an offer or a recorded decline.

Offers are a free month, a discount for a number of months, or a free upgrade. Each can be turned off in Settings. The price comes from the catalog when a product is chosen, otherwise from the current subscription. A discount over that person's cap, or more than the free months a supervisor may approve, waits in the approvals inbox. One free month is the starting limit. An accepted free month posts a credit. A discount sets a new subscription price and leaves old invoices alone.

A health score under 50 adds an at-risk tag and one task. A terminated service gets one win-back task after the number of days in Settings, starting at 75. The save rate is the share of closed cases that were saved. Churn reasons are the comma-separated list in Settings.

The figures are listed in [to-confirm.md](to-confirm.md).

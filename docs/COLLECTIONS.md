# Collections

Reminders follow the dates in Settings: three days before the service ends, on the day, three days after, a suspend request at seven days, and a termination request at thirty days. The suspend request also waits out the grace days. Each step is sent once for that service period.

WhatsApp is a link on a task, and only when the customer has agreed. Otherwise the task says there is no WhatsApp. Email goes out when an address is on file.

The board groups people by stage and by how long the case has been open. A promise to pay creates a follow-up task. The day after the promise date, the case becomes a broken promise.

Suspending a service, and asking to turn it back on, creates a task for Krypton. Krypton is not connected. A payment that clears the balance of a suspended customer queues that reactivation task. A reconnection fee, if Settings is above zero, is a new invoice. Marking the line reactivated is a person confirming the Krypton change.

A customer with an approved exemption is not suspended automatically. That skip is in the audit log. A write-off still goes through the approvals inbox.

The renewal list is every prepaid service ending inside the window in Settings. A service that does not renew itself also gets one task inside the shorter window.

The figures are listed in [to-confirm.md](to-confirm.md).

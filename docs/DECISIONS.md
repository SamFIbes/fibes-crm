# Decisions

Answers Sam gave in chat. These are the working rules until he changes them. The product screens that use them are not built yet. The values also live in `config/fibes.php` so later work does not invent a second list.

## 31 October 2026

### What FIBES sells (question 1)

Services:

- Fiber, in two forms: FTTH and FTTB
- PTMP
- PTP
- Internal Setup
- DSL

HSI is not a service type. Reseller is a kind of customer, not a service.

### How fast problems should be handled (question 2)

Sam’s words: resolution is usually 1–2 days. The starting targets, which Settings will be able to change:

| Priority | First response | Resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | 15 minutes | 24 hours |
| P2 | 30 minutes | 24 hours |
| P3 | 2 hours | 48 hours |
| P4 | 8 hours | 48 hours |

The response times are the earlier working assumption. The resolve times replace the shorter ones from that assumption. A new lead’s first response stays 15 minutes.

### When the clock runs (question 3)

Support is 24/7. The SLA clock uses calendar time, including nights and weekends. It does not pause outside an office day.

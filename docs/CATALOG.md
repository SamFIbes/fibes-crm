# Catalog

Plans, add-ons, equipment, and one-time fees live here. The page is Catalog in the money section of the menu.

## Prices

Every price is in US dollars, with the Lebanese pound shown from the day’s exchange rate and rounded to the nearest thousand. A price belongs to a product, starts on a date, and can be limited to a customer type or a town. A blank customer type means everyone. A more specific row wins: a town beats a customer type, and a customer type beats the general price. When two rows are equally specific, the later start date wins.

A price is never edited or deleted. Publishing a new price adds a row. Services, and later quotes and invoices, keep the price they were given. The button that would move existing services onto a new price is present and refused until the approval inbox exists.

Finance can request a price. The person who asked cannot publish it. Admin publishes it, which writes the new price row.

The starter fiber plan is `SMN-8MB UP TO 16MB` at $35, and $40 for a business customer. Installation, relocation, reconnection, a static IP, and a fiber terminal are seeded too. Each of those prices is marked “To confirm by Sam”.

## Promotions

A code can be a percent, a fixed amount, free months, free installation, or free equipment. It has dates, a maximum number of uses, a per-customer limit, and who it applies to. Codes do not combine unless each one is marked as combinable. Otherwise only the larger saving is used. An expired code is refused. A code that needs approval is shown but does not change the price yet. A promotion can be attached to a campaign.

## Discount limits

Each role has a percent and a dollar cap, per line and per quote. Sales agents are at 5 percent and $5 on a line, and 5 percent and $10 on a quote. Sales managers are at 15 percent and $25, and 15 percent and $50. Call-center agents are at zero. These caps are not enforced until quotes exist. They are marked to confirm.

## Who can see what

Admin edits products, publishes prices, and edits promotions. Sales managers, sales agents, and the call center can see plans and list prices. Management, finance, and the auditor can view. Finance requests prices and can see cost. Cost is also visible to admin and management, and is left out of the product list for everyone else. Warehouse staff cannot open the page.

## Picking a plan

The customer form offers the public plans. Choosing one stores the plan name, the speed, and the price for that customer’s type on that day. Typing a different monthly amount does not override the catalog price.

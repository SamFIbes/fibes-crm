# Dashboards and reports

Each person lands on a home page for their work: sales, the supervisor, the agent, operations, the network, finance, the warehouse, or management. A tile opens the report, and the report lists the records it counted, with a link to that list.

Who can open a report follows the role. Management, an admin, and an auditor can open all of them. Finance opens finance and customers. Sales opens sales. The call center opens calls, and a supervisor also opens support. Operations opens field work. The warehouse opens stock. Anyone else is refused. A sales agent sees only their own rows. A manager sees the team. An export uses the same limit, and a person without export permission cannot download.

A download can be a CSV file, a spreadsheet, or a PDF. It is recorded in the audit log. The link stops working after the number of hours in Settings, which starts at 24. More rows than the queue limit are built in the background. Each person can download only so many reports an hour. That limit starts at 10.

The home page looks back 30 days unless the dates are changed, and it refreshes every 30 seconds. A person can save which tiles stay on their home. Supervisors and sales managers get a short email at 08:00, and again on Monday. The hour, the weekday, and the roles are in Settings.

Reverb is still not installed, so nothing on this page uses a live socket.

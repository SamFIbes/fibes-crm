# Changelog

## 2026-11-12

The dashboard now shows campaign results. For leads added in the dates you pick, it lists each campaign with its leads, deals, wins, won value and how many leads turned into a deal or a win, plus cost per lead and per win when the campaign has a budget. It also compares campaign leads with leads that came in without a campaign.

# Changelog

## 2026-11-15

A new Onboarding board follows internet clients from the first sales handoff until the service is active. Cards can be created automatically from sales leads, or with one click from a lead or customer page, and they show the source salesperson. Staff can drag or move cards between stages, set an assignee and a follow-up date, and see stale cards and follow-up counts in the side menu.

Pull request: https://github.com/SamFIbes/fibes-crm/pull/49

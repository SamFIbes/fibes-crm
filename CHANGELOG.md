# Changelog

## 2026-11-12

There is a Support desk under Tickets. Staff can work through an inbox of tickets, a queue of tickets waiting for a public reply (with two suggested replies), a customer profile drawer, and simple charts for ticket volume and first-response times. The existing ticket list and ticket page are unchanged. Suggested replies are canned templates, not generated by a model, and they use the ticket and customer already on the record.

Pull request: https://github.com/SamFIbes/fibes-crm/pull/42
